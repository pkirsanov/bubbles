## Description
A short, funny animated cartoon overview of Bubbles: the tiny department that turns AI coding chaos into structured work with specialists, artifacts, gates, and proof.

This is the quick shareable version, drawn in the same park-office dispatch-map style as the Bubbles diagrams. Moving characters carry the work through lanes, artifact cards travel with the job, gates stamp the evidence, and “done” means more than “the agent sounded confident.”
